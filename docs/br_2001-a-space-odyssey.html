2001: A Space Odyssey — Arthur C. Clarke

  • Rating

  • Review

    Beautifully atmospheric book with a mind-bending ending. I can instantly see why this book has gone down as a classic. I've seen complaints that this book is too slow. I disagree and feel the atmosphere takes time to craft and is essential to the feel of the book. Completely faultless except…

    Sexism. I know this book was written in a different time but nonetheless the sexism wasn't justified in any way. Considering the thought that must have been given to the beautiful prose, there are no excuses for the flagrant misogyny.

    For that and only that, this book loses a star.
