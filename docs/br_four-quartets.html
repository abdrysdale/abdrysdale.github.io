  • Review

    This is my first foray into poetry and I am hooked. I've never read anything so beautifully written and yet with such profound meaning.

    It's like Eliot has taken a beautifully written several thousand pages long book about religion, philosophy and life, before maturing and distilling it into the Four Quartets. I could spend hours pondering single phrases and still never truly grasp their significance.
