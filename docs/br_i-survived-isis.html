I Survived ISIS — Roza Alomar

  • Rating

    5.0/5 ★★★★★

  • Review

    Thank you NetGalley and Dundurn Press for sending this book for review consideration. All opinions are my own.

    I have never read anything about anyone so brave as the people in this book. Not only that, but how Roza had the courage to put all of this to paper is beyond anything I can comprehend. I've never cried more at reading a book; nor have I read anything as remotely powerful.

    I will be buying and rereading this book to ensure my "1st world" problems remain grounded in reality.

    Moreover, I cannot fathom how suffering like this can exist whilst people of power and influence do nothing except sleep like a dragon, guarding their hoard.
