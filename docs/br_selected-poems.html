Selected Poems — Rainer Maria Rilke

  • Rating

    5.0/5 ★★★★★

  • Review

    It's said that 20th century poets (such as Rilke) ushered in an era whereby more was demanded from the reader. Unfortunately, I fell short in this regard.

    Each poem was truly crafted. Fragile, insomuch as every word, space and punctuation had a very delicate place on the page, and yet strong - befitting of the weight of the words. A lot went over my head but all of the poems I will read time and time again to try to obtain a glimmer of Rilke's insight.

    The collection was beautifully organised, allowing the reader to join Rilke in his maturation. I would have liked more notes on the choices made the translator - for instance "blick" being translated to "gaze". But the beauty of the poems outweighs all and this remains a 5 star read.
