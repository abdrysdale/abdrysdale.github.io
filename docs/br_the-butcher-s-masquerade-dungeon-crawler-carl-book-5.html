  • Rating

    5.0/5 ★★★★★

  • Review

    If you've made it this far in the series you know you'll love this book. Much stronger than the last in terms of world building but that may be personal preference as I love the intricate fantasy worlds on floors 3, 6 and 9.

    What really stood out for me in this book were the minor characters. I think it is a credit to Matt that I would gladly pick up a book from any of the side characters' perspective. As the wit fades in the writing, thought provoking social and cultural analysis pervades.
