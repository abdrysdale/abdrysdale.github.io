    This book genuinely had me hooked from page 1. I finished this so quickly and then immediately picked up book 2.

    I'm a big fan of King's writing style and I think he writes brilliant characters, clever plots and I was not disappointed here. What stood for me was the depth of the world. I couldn't recommend this book enough for those who like grim dark epic high fantasy.
