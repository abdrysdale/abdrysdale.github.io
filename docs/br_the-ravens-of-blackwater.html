The Ravens of Blackwater — Edward Marston

  • Rating

    2.0/5 ★★☆☆☆ (DNF at 80 pages)

  • Review

    I bought this as a blind date with a book from Costa. I don't want to be too harsh as it cost less than the coffee — although the coffee had more character development.

    The premise was really interesting and I felt like the author knew his stuff about the historical context. That being said, the characters felt incredibly flat and I can't be sustained on plot alone.

    I've definitely read a lot of worse books but life is too short for okay books.
