  • Review

    I'm afraid I can't lend you this book as it's water damaged from my tears. An absolutely fantastic read. I have barely been able to put it down.

    I could not recommend it more.

    My only complaint is the length, I needed more. When comparing it to another book of a similar calibre, "The Heart's Invisible Furies", what held this book back for me was the secondary characters. They were brilliant but they could have been explorered so much further. It could have easily been double the length. And it's this reason why Evelyn Hugo will always a space in my heart, but sadly the room is a little smaller than it could have been.
