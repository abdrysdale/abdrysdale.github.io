  • Review

    I didn't click any of the Fable tags for this book and that pretty much sums it up. Very middle of the road.

    Considering this book is by a comic, I didn't really laugh out loud at any bits but it did give me a little chuckle here and there. Don't get me wrong, I'm a huge fan of her comedy but the book didn't click in the same way. Perhaps what I like most about French is her physical comedy which didn't translate well into a book.

    I liked the general message of celebrating imperfections but it felt watered down by the need to end with some further funny stories.

    Overall, this book must have been very cathartic for Dawn French but nothing special to read.
