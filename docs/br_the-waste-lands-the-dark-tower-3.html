  • Rating

  • Review

    Wow. What. A. Book. The world of the Dark Tower is like no other I've ever read and I can't wait to read the next in the series. I was like putty in Stephen King's hands.

    Immersive, suspenseful, emotional and mind bending.

    *As a side note, I'd say this book is a lot darker than the previous two. So if you've read the first two books and found them a little too grim you may want to brace yourself for this book. For this reason I will need a couple of palette cleansers before I continue.
