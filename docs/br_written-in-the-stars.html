Written in the Stars — Aisha Saeed

  • Rating

    4.5/5 ★★★★½

  • Review

    Currently about to go into theatre for heart surgery. BECAUSE THIS BOOK RIPPED IT TO SHREDS. I'm honestly heartbroken. Nay, soul-broken. Nay, essence-of-human-existence-transcending-time broken. Actually too far - just soul-broken. But what an emotionally powerful book.

    The plot didn't go where I expected it to go and the whole book felt so believable, so real. I was on the edge of my seat numerous times. I will definitely read more by this author.

    The only thing that lets this book down for me is that some of the secondary characters felt underdeveloped.

    Also, it's worth noting that even though this is a YA book there are definitely very hard themes in this book - be prepared.

    Will be recommending this to anyone who seems too cheerful.
