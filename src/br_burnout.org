* Burnout --- Emily Nagoski, PhD

** Rating

5.0/5 ★★★★★

** Review

I would recommend this book to anyone and everyone who has been stressed.

Although aimed at people who identify as women it is applicable to all and essential reading for anyone who benefits from gender privilege.

I'm slightly worried that if Emily Nagoski writes a book for all genders it will actually be applicable to all sentient beings or she'll create a grand unified theory of the universe.
