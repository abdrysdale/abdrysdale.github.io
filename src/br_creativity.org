* Creativity --- John Cleese

** Rating

4.0/5 ★★★★☆

** Review

This book was too short to attain a 5 star rating for me. I don't think it was ever intended to be a piece of literary excellence, rather advice from John's years of experience of being creative.

There's a sprinkling of research and a smattering of accounts with experts which add more substance to the book.

Overall, the subject of creativity was eloquently and humorously touched upon - yielding some very useful advice!

If you've got a spare hour or so I'd recommend reading/listening to it to really get you thinking about how and what it means to be creative.  However, if you've read about the subject matter then I doubt this book will offer new content, rather a creative narrative.
