* Fitness Weight Training (Fitness Spectrum Series) --- Thomas R. Baechle

** Rating

3.5/5 ★★★½☆

** Review

Accessible, mostly scientific, well structured and fat phobic.

I imagine that a lot of books in this style exhibit fat phobia as they believe that's what their readers want to read - or perhaps what they should read if they're going to spend more money on fitness products.

Nonetheless, it's well structured and makes it very easy to construct a training plan.
