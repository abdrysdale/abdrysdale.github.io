* How Not To Be a Boy --- Robert Webb

** Rating

5.0/5 ★★★★★

** Review

Came for the love of peep show, stayed to dismantle the patriarchy.

A brilliant and insightful read - I'll be recommending it to anyone and everyone who wants (or needs) to understand the role we're assigned at birth dictates our entire life.

As a biography, it was also refreshing original and honest - I adored the non-linear/time-travelling narrative!

Couldn't fault it!
