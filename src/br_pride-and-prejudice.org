* Pride and Prejudice --- Jane Austen

** Rating

5.0/5 ★★★★★

** Review

The fact that Jane Austen was born 250 years ago this year and this book had me laughing out loud is a true testament to her humour.

I picked this book from my TBR Jar prompt "A book of one your favourite films that you've never read". I'd previously been put off by the language but Austin's vision flows so naturally that I become instantly immersed. One of my favourite films and now one of my favourite books. The King Pin of Cloth Bound Classics.
