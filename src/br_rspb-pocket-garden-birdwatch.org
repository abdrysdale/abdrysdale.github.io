* RSPB Pocket Garden Birdwatch --- Mark Ward

** Rating

4.5/5 ★★★★½

** Review

A lovely reference filled with everything you'd want to know to get started with watching birds in your garden.

There was some assumed knowledge (such as the diameter of a circle) which makes the book a bit less accessable for some. Similarly, bird sizes were only displayed in the summary chart and not on the species profile.

Overall I'd recommend it to anyone who has a garden!
