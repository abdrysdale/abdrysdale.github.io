** Review

No matter where you are when you read it, this collection of tales scoops you up, wraps you in a woollen blanket, sets you gently down next to friends & family, rests your head against a loved ones shoulder, lights a log fire before embodying a grandparent who captivates all with stories from years gone by.

TL;DR It's in the night garden for adults.
