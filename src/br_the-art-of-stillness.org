* The Art of Stillness --- Pico Iyer

** Rating

4.0/5 ★★★★☆

** Review

Lovely book on the art of stillness. 

One complaint for me is that this book felt too easily understood. Counterintuitive for most genres but, personally, when reading zen for philosophy, I like to be confused and have the confusion of a story sit with me for many hours. Then, when I'm least expecting have an epiphany. It's this intangible aspect that gives zen philosophy it's depth.
I hear your retort already "But this is an introductory book?! A novella to wet the appetite!". If this was so, where were the practical applications average person? How can the single working mother find time for a hour of meditation?

This complaint, however, does not detract from the timely and beautifully simple message of the book. Be still.
