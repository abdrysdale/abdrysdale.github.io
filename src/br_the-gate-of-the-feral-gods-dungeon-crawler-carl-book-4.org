* The Gate of the Feral Gods: Dungeon Crawler Carl Book 4 --- Matt Dinniman

** Rating

5.0/5 ★★★★★

** Review

If you read the DCC books up until now you have a pretty good idea of whether you like the series or not. And having read the first 3 and considering the 4th, you probably like the series - rather than not. So I've come to the conclusion that I'm only writing this review for myself.

The character depth and world building really shone out for me in this book. Not just the literal building of the game world but the politics of the surrounding universe. The humour was on point as per and the plot was as gripping as ever. Can't fault it.
