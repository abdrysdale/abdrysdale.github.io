** Rating

5.0/5 ★★★★★

** Review

It was lovely to return Janloon since reading the Green Bone Saga. This beautifully written novella sucks you into the green bone world with excellent characters following the solving of a mystery.

If you loved the Green Bone Saga then you'll like love the nostalgia returning to Kekon to a time before Jade City.
