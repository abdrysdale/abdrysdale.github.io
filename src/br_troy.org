** Review

What a book. Stephen Fry brings the ancient world to life with his vivid and memorable story telling. In spite of knowing the story I was hooked from start to finish.

If you haven't read the others in this series, start with Mythos, Heros and then Troy. I rarely reread books but this serious will definitely be one I return to again and again.
