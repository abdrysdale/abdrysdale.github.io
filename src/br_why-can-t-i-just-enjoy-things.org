* Why Can't I Just Enjoy Things? --- Pierre Novellie

** Rating

3.5/5 ★★★½☆

** Review

Insightful, humoures and well written. I wish I'd read this book a decade ago - not that it existed then. Genuinely had me laughing out loud and also thinking deeply about my life.

Loses marks for me due to the subpar handling of autistic mental health - which lacked a personable touch. The book was also too detailed for me and I feel like there's an art in choosing what not to write which was not achieved during this book. Although I'd expect nothing less in a book by an autistic author brain dumping their special interest.
