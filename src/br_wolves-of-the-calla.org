* Wolves of the Calla --- Stephen King

** Rating

5.0/5 ★★★★★

** Review

This book is about 95% build up and about 5% of action and I absolutely loved it.

Stephen King beautifully crafted a cast of characters and backstory that meant I was think so much about this book when I wasn't reading it.

Slower pace than the other books but meticulously sculpted.
